How it
works

We meet by video, anywhere in the world, from the comfort of your own home. Using easy collaboration software, we work on your documents together, at the same time, not by passing files back and forth.

I assign the homework and you drive the speed of the engagement. You get direct access to my calendar, so you schedule our time when you are ready and in slots that work for you.

What we create together
Your story

Unique Value Propositions

We go through your history and future, turn scattered data into information, and synthesize it into powerful, true stories you can tell with gusto.

Your message

Pitch & brand

Elevator pitches, a ten-word brand for your skill set, and answers to "tell me about yourself" that land in two minutes or less.

Your toolkit

Search tools & process

Resumé, LinkedIn, personality profiling, True North statements, and a modern pursuit process. Professional headshots and video assets when you are ready.

Flexible terms

Because I have been laid off too, I built terms that respect your situation.

You pay an upfront retainer to get started, and half of that is refundable. After that, you are not invoiced again until you land. At that point I invoice a small portion of your first year's salary. We both have skin in the game.
