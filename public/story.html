My story

Hi, I'm Don. Here's how I got here.

I do this work because I have lived it. I know exactly what it feels like to be a senior leader who suddenly has to explain their worth to strangers.

It was just before Halloween in 2003. I was in my office, getting ready for the day. I loved my job, and I loved the company I did it for.

Around 8:30 that morning my phone rang. It was my boss. There was going to be a company-wide reduction, and I was one of the people being separated. After more than 16 years of award-winning performance and a lot of valuable contributions, I was laid off.

I did not see it coming. And worse, I had to learn how to look for a job all over again. The last time I had done that on purpose was 1984.

Back then, looking for a job was simple. You found an ad in the local paper, typed up your resumé, and mailed it in. A company might receive ten or twelve resumés and read every one. By 2003, the internet had changed everything, and standing out had become the whole game. Today a person is shown well over twelve thousand marketing messages a day. To be found, you have to truly resonate.

How I got started

I was frustrated and I did not know where to begin, so I did something. I started attending networking meetings, a lot of them. I also joined a well-known job-search ministry in the northwest suburbs of Chicago, led by a former VP of HR for a major aircraft manufacturer. I asked to help, joined the core team, and was welcomed in.

Every week that group hosted speakers with different perspectives on job search. I took meticulous notes and asked endless questions. Over the next fifteen years I learned a great deal about how this process really works.

I started helping friends, then friends of friends. One long email of advice kept growing until I finally turned it into a book about job search. It is for sale now, and I use it as the handbook inside my engagements.

My mission

My job is to coax out of you the content you need to tell a powerful story, and to help you tell it in compelling fashion.

I focus on senior leaders because there are fewer chairs at the top. The higher you go, the more you need to know, and truly appreciate, the unique contributions you have made. Then you need to tell that story so it resonates with an audience: quickly, concisely, and consistently.

I also work with small and medium businesses on the other side of the table, helping them hire well and take care of people they have to let go.
