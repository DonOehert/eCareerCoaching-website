FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things executives ask me most before we decide to work together.

Who do you work with?

Senior leaders in transition, mostly Directors through the C-suite, and usually 45 and up. If you have run teams, owned outcomes, and carried real responsibility, you are who I built this for.

What makes your approach different?

Two things. First, I specialize in ageism, so we address it head on instead of pretending it is not in the room. Second, we lead with outcomes, not responsibilities. Responsibilities get listed. Outcomes get hired.

How does the 1:1 coaching work?

We meet by video, wherever you are, and build your story and your search tools together. There are two ways in: Executive Essentials, a guided, hybrid track that pairs a few focused sessions with the tools you need, and Executive Advantage, a three month white glove partnership where we co-create your resumes, stories, and interview prep together.

Do you offer flexible payment?

Yes. I know a transition is exactly when money feels tightest, so I keep the arrangements flexible, including options that let you pay as you land. We will find something that works.

Do you really help with ageism?

It is my specialty. The goal is not to hide your experience or shave years off your resume. It is to make your age irrelevant by making your value obvious. When they see the number you moved, they stop doing math on the year you were born.

Do you work with employers?

Yes. For small and midsize companies I help with hiring and with outplacement, so you either bring the right person on board or take care of the people you have to let go. See the For Employers page for more.

Is there a do-it-yourself option?

Yes. The Resume Reality Check gives you guardrailed prompts and a fillable workbook that tailor your resume to any role without inventing a thing. It is a good start if you want to move on your own first.

How do we start?

With a short, no-pressure call. We figure out whether we are a fit, and if we are not, I will point you in the right direction. Book it from the button up top.
