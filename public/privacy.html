Privacy

Privacy policy.

How eCareerCoaching.com handles the information you share. Last updated October 1, 2026.

This site is run by Don Oehlert, eCareerCoaching.com. I keep this simple: I collect only what I need to help you, and I do not sell your information to anyone.

What I collect

When you book a call, send an email, or join my list, I receive what you choose to share, typically your name, email address, and anything you write to me. When you book through the scheduler, that service collects the details needed to set up the meeting. Like most websites, mine also records basic, non-identifying traffic data so I can see what is useful.

How I use it

To reply to you, prepare for and hold our calls, deliver anything you have signed up for, and send occasional emails if you have asked to hear from me. That is all.

The services I rely on

A few trusted tools help me run the business, and your information passes through them only for that purpose: the scheduler I use to book calls, the email service I use to send messages you have opted into, my client management system, and the host that serves this website, and the AI service that powers the Résumé Check. Each has its own privacy terms.

The automated Résumé Check

When you use the Résumé Check, your résumé is sent to Anthropic, the company behind the Claude AI model, so it can write your draft review. It is used only for that purpose, under Anthropic's own privacy terms. I do not store your résumé, and I never see it unless you send it to me yourself. Your name and email are added to my email list, and you can unsubscribe anytime. To limit misuse, the site keeps a scrambled (hashed) version of your email address and network address for about two days, used only to count how many checks have been run.

Email

I only email people who have asked to hear from me. Every message has an unsubscribe link, and you can leave anytime. See the Unsubscribe page.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Write to info@eCareerCoaching.com and I will take care of it.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@eCareerCoaching.com.
