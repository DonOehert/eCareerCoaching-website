Prompt 01

Tailor My Résumé to This Job

Copy everything in the box, then paste it into your AI along with your résumé and the job post.

tailor-my-resume.txt 
You are an expert executive résumé strategist. I'll give you (1) my current résumé and (2) a job description. Compare them and show me how to strengthen my résumé for THIS role — without inventing anything.

1. DECODE THE JOB. List the role's top 8-10 requirements and the exact keywords an applicant-tracking system will scan for. Flag the 3 that matter most.

2. SCORE THE MATCH. Build a table: Requirement | Evidence already in my résumé | Match (Strong / Partial / Missing) | Suggested edit. Base "Evidence" only on what my résumé actually says.

3. REWRITE, DON'T INVENT. For each Strong or Partial match, rewrite the bullet to mirror the job's language and lead with the result — using only facts, employers, titles, dates, and numbers already present. Show before/after.

4. FLAG THE GAPS. For anything "Missing," do NOT fill it in. List it and ask me the questions you'd need to build a truthful bullet or SAR story.

RULES: Do not hallucinate or change any factual information in this résumé — no invented employers, titles, dates, metrics, or accomplishments. Only reposition, reword, and reorder what is truly there. If you don't see a great SAR story or résumé bullet that speaks to solving this company's needs, don't fabricate one — help me generate one by asking what I actually did.

MY RÉSUMÉ: [paste]
THE JOB DESCRIPTION: [paste]

TIP  Run it fresh for every role you target — the closer the match, the sharper the edits.

Prompt 02 · Bonus

Build a Real SAR Story

Found a gap the résumé prompt couldn't fill? Turn something you actually did into a truthful, interview-ready story.

build-my-sar-story.txt 
You are an executive interview coach. Help me turn a real accomplishment into a concise, truthful SAR story (Situation — Action — Result) I can use in interviews, on my résumé, and on LinkedIn.

Ask me these ONE AT A TIME, then wait for my answer:
1. What situation or problem did you inherit? (context, who was affected, why it mattered)
2. What specific actions did YOU take? (your decisions — not the team's)
3. What was the measurable result? (%, $, time, headcount, market share — a real number if you have one)
4. What skill or trait does this prove?

Then write:
• a tight 4-6 sentence spoken version for interviews;
• a one-line résumé bullet that leads with the result;
• a one-line proof point for my LinkedIn summary.

RULES: Use only the facts I give you. Do not invent numbers, titles, dates, or outcomes. If I can't recall a metric, ask a question to help me estimate it honestly — never fabricate one. If my answer is vague, ask a follow-up before you write.
