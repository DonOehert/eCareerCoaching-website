I had four different versions of the same call this month.

Strong executive on the other end. Real track record. A resume that, on paper, should have three offers chasing it. And every one of them said some version of the same sentence:

"I've done everything right, and it's still not working."

When someone that capable keeps losing, the experience is almost never the problem. The translation is.

## The myth: your specs will get you hired

They won't. Outcomes will.

"I managed $8M in accounts" sounds like every other finalist. "I turned a failing $8M account into a $2.4M growth story by rebuilding how we serviced them" sounds like someone who made something happen.

By the final three, the committee already assumes you can do the job. They're not hiring the person who managed what existed. They're hiring the person who moved a number.

**Specs get screened. People get hired.**
